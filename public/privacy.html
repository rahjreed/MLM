Privacy Notice

Updated November 13, 2026

This notice explains information handling on the No Time to Share educational and referral website.

The free quiz

Your quiz answers are used in your browser to produce an immediate travel profile. The quiz does not require your name or email to show a result. The current website does not send those answers to a database as part of the quiz itself.

Optional email updates

If you choose to subscribe, we request your first name, email address and affirmative consent. We submit your information to our email service provider, Kit, to manage messages and unsubscribe requests. The signup may require email confirmation. We do not treat you as subscribed unless Kit confirms the signup request.

Other service providers

Our hosting provider may process normal technical information such as IP addresses and request logs needed to serve the website. Embedded videos and external referral links may load content from their own providers, who may process information under their own privacy notices. Following our Travorium referral link takes you to an independent provider with its own policies.

Your choices

You may skip the email signup and still see quiz results. If you join our mailing list, you may unsubscribe using the link in any email. For inquiries about personal information, reply to a message sent by the No Time to Share mailing list or use your existing No Time to Share contact channel.

Changes

We may update this notice as the website and its services evolve. The updated date on this page will change accordingly.
