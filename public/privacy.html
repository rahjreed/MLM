Privacy Notice

Updated November 13, 2026

This notice explains information handling on the No Time to Share educational and referral website.

The free quiz

Your quiz answers are used in your browser to produce an immediate travel profile. You can answer all six questions without providing your contact details. To unlock your personalized results, the website requests your email address and consent to be contacted by email about the assessment, vacation options and relevant travel tips. Your results appear in your browser, not as an automatically generated email report. Your quiz answers and traveler profile stay in your browser.

Email signup and consent

To unlock results, we request your email address and affirmative consent to travel-related emails. We submit your information to our email service provider, Kit, to manage messages and unsubscribe requests. The website shows your results once Kit accepts your signup.

Other service providers

Our hosting provider may process normal technical information such as IP addresses and request logs needed to serve the website. Embedded videos and external referral links may load content from their own providers, who may process information under their own privacy notices. Following our Travorium referral link takes you to an independent provider with its own policies.

Your choices

You may complete the questions without contact information, but results are gated behind the signup form. If you join our mailing list, you may unsubscribe using the link in any email. For inquiries about personal information, reply to a message sent by the No Time to Share mailing list or use your existing No Time to Share contact channel.

Changes

We may update this notice as the website and its services evolve. The updated date on this page will change accordingly.
